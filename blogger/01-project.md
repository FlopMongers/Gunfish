# Gunfish — Project Overview

## What is Gunfish?

Gunfish is a local multiplayer 2D arcade fighting game built in Unity (2021 LTS). Players control segmented, physics-based fish armed with a variety of weapons and compete in several game modes across hand-crafted arenas. The game was designed for a custom arcade cabinet and debuted at MAGFest 2024, with a second showing at MAGFest 2025.

The project is developed by the FlopMongers team (3+ developers). Active development began in January 2023 and has been ongoing through January 2025.

---

## Genre and Platform

- **Genre:** Local multiplayer arcade fighting/party game
- **Engine:** Unity 2021 LTS (2D)
- **Platform:** Windows (built for a custom arcade cabinet, "The Gunfish Rectangle")
- **Players:** 2–4 local players via gamepads
- **Input:** Unity New Input System with custom Arduino cabinet integration

---

## Core Concept

Each player selects a fish character and a weapon. Fish are segmented, physics-driven bodies rendered via LineRenderer — they flop, bounce, and fly through the air using physics forces. Players flop to move on land, swim through water zones, and fire weapons to damage opponents. Matches cycle through multiple arenas with a scoreboard between rounds.

---

## Game Modes

1. **Deathmatch** — Free-for-all or team elimination. Each player has 3 stocks (lives). Last fish standing wins the round. Pelicans spawn after a timeout to push the action.
2. **Race** — Checkpoint-based progression through a level. First to cross all checkpoints wins. Time and DNF (did not finish) are tracked.
3. **Bassball** — A team sport mode. Two teams of fish try to knock a ball into the opposing team's goal. Goal-based scoring across rounds.

## Levels / Arenas

Levels are Unity scenes loaded at runtime by the LevelManager. Each level has spawn points, environmental hazards, and optional water zones. Levels include:

- **Barrel** — A pirate ship barrel arena with dynamic physics objects, lanterns, and chains
- **Crags** — Rocky terrain with lighting effects and igneous rocks
- **Cargo Hold** — Ship interior with crates and hazards
- **Great Bay** — Coastal level with water, currents, a turtle, and kelp
- **Valley** — Mountainous arena with varied terrain
- **Pipes** — Tight corridors with broken pipes and kill boxes
- **Firing Range** — Practice/test level
- **Beach** — Sandy shore with water, beachball, cannon, and turtle
- **Acid Factory** — Industrial level with acid water, conveyors, cannons, and a button-activated platform
- **Bomb Valley** — Explosive barrel-heavy level

Levels use tiled terrain sprites (metal, wood, sand, rocks) with matching border tiles, added in the MAGFest 2025 cycle.

---

## Level Objects and Hazards

- **Water zones** — Dynamic water surface with wave propagation, buoyancy, splash effects. Fish swim underwater with different physics. Acid water variant deals damage over time.
- **Explosives** — Explosive barrels, sea mines (bump-triggered)
- **Cannons** — Directional launchers that shoot fish with flame effects
- **Moving platforms** — B-spline-pathed platforms that can pause at waypoints
- **Pelicans** — Flying enemies that attack fish after a timeout in deathmatch
- **Sea urchins** — Spiky contact damage hazards
- **Boxer crabs** — Melee combat enemies
- **Toasters** — Heating hazards
- **Fish hooks** — Line-based catching mechanics
- **Crates** — Breakable containers that can drop powerups
- **Pinball flippers/plungers** — Spring-based launch mechanics
- **Conveyor belts** — Directional movement zones
- **Big buttons** — Pressure-activated triggers

---

## Powerups

- **Invincibility** — Temporary damage immunity
- **Health Pickup** — Restores health
- **Sharkmode** — Doubles underwater force/velocity, damages non-sharkmode fish on contact, triggers special music

---

## Key Features

- **Physics-based fish** — Segmented bodies with hinge joints, procedurally generated by GunfishGenerator
- **Custom fish shader** — Supports player color outlines, alpha, pulsing border effects
- **Smart Lighting 2D** — Dynamic 2D lighting via FunkyCode plugin
- **Marquee announcer** — Scrolling text with voice quips for kills, selections, countdowns, goals
- **Freeze frame + camera shake** — Impact effects on kills
- **Offscreen tracker** — Arrow indicators for off-camera fish
- **Music system** — Menu/gameplay/sharkmode track sets with crossfading
- **Arduino cabinet integration** — Serial communication for cabinet LED effects driven by audio loudness
- **Attractors** — Idle-mode animations on the cabinet splash screen
- **Stats/telemetry** — SQLite-based logging of matches, damage events, spawns, powerup pickups (added for MAGFest 2025)
- **Parallax backgrounds** — Multi-layer scrolling sky and scenery
- **DOTween animations** — Used throughout UI and effects
